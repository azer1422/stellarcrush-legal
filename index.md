# Privacy Policy — Stellar Crush Universe

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

Stellar Crush Universe ("the game") is developed by Nexus Applications
("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles
and where it goes. The short version: we run no accounts and no
servers of our own, and we collect as little as possible.

## Information we do NOT collect

The game has no sign-up, no login, and no profile. We never ask for
your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location, and the game
does not read them.

## Game progress

Your level progress, scores, stars, streaks, and settings are stored
on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, the game also stores a
copy in your private iCloud key-value storage so your progress follows
you to your other devices. That data lives in YOUR iCloud account,
governed by Apple's terms; we cannot read it and operate no server
that receives it.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no card
numbers or billing details. Purchase receipts are validated on-device
through Apple's StoreKit.

## Crash reporting (Sentry)

If the game crashes, technical details about the crash (device model,
OS version, the state of the code at the moment of the crash) are sent
to Sentry, our crash-reporting processor, so we can fix bugs. Crash
reports also include coarse progress markers (for example, that a
player has passed a given level number) so we know when to add more
content. These reports contain no name, email, or advertising
identifier. Sentry's privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows an optional rewarded ad ("watch an ad for a life")
served by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may process device
identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as
described in Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy. On iOS, personalized advertising
uses the advertising identifier ONLY if you allow tracking in the App
Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, ads are non-personalized.

## Children

The game is playable by all ages and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address
